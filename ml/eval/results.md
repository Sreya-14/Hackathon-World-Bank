## Speech to text: FLEURS Malayalam (test split)

Clean read speech, so real outdoor voice notes will do worse. Malayalam fuses words, so one wrong letter makes a whole word wrong: character error rate (CER) is the fairer number.

| Model | Clips | CER | WER | Median seconds per clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vrclc/Whisper-small-Malayalam` | 150 | 14.1% | 47.3% | 14.27 |

- FLEURS records each sentence with several speakers, so the first N clips cover fewer distinct sentences.
- The model's own confidence stays near 0.98 even at 14% CER, so "not sure" relies on the other checks (script, translation length, offer check, numbers), not on confidence.
- Vanilla `openai/whisper-small` writes Malayalam in Latin letters ("Paa Amm Oil, anna dalida maya…") and is unusable for this; spot-checked on FLEURS clips, not scored.

## Speed and cost

- Speech to text: median 14.27 s per FLEURS clip; the clips are 13.4 s long at the median (5.6–31.6 s), so about real time on this CPU.
- Translation + listing + German + Malayalam read-back: median 30.7 s per listing.
- No paid API and no GPU: cost per listing is only server CPU time (seconds above × the server's price per second). The plan's 30 s target is not met on this laptop CPU.

## Known limits

- Translation errors pass through: "Kerala meals served" became "Kerala will be handed over to my house", pepper became "corn"/"pumpkin". Numbers are checked automatically; other invented details are not, which is why the host hears the listing in Malayalam and approves it.
- Place names outside `ml/pipeline/places.py` can be mistranslated (Edakkal → "gravel cave").
- NLLB-200 and MMS-TTS are non-commercial (CC-BY-NC-4.0); a commercial version needs other models.
- Not yet tested on real voice notes from a host.
